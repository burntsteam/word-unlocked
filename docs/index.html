Word Unlocked

Put scripture on your Lock Screen. Offline and private.

Word Unlocked shows a Bible verse on your iPhone Lock Screen. Choose from Daily Verse, Weekly Theme, Topic, Chapter, Memorization, or Favorites modes, and pick a translation — five of them work fully offline, with no account, no ads, no tracking, and no analytics.

Offline by default

King James Version, World English Bible, Berean Standard Bible, American Standard Version, and Literal Standard Version are bundled in the app and never require a network connection.

Nothing collected

No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Word Unlocked's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected."

Two optional live translations

The English Standard Version and the Recovery Version are fetched live when you select them, then cached on your device.

Built for the Lock Screen

Inline, circular, and rectangular widget styles designed for iOS Lock Screen customization.
