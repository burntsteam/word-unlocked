Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Word Unlocked at a glance:

  • No account required
  • No ads
  • No tracking
  • No analytics
  • No cloud sync
  • No AI processing
  • No third-party SDKs
  • Five translations work offline

Data Collection

No data is ever collected. Word Unlocked does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no crash reporting.

This is reflected in the app's Apple Privacy Manifest and its App Store product page, where Word Unlocked's App Privacy is declared as "Data Not Collected."

Network Access

Word Unlocked bundles five Bible translations that work entirely offline. One additional translation is available live, and it only makes a network request when you actually select it.

Offline translations

King James Version (KJV), World English Bible (WEB), Berean Standard Bible (BSB), American Standard Version (ASV), and Literal Standard Version (LSV) are bundled with the app and stored entirely on your device. No network request is ever made when one of these translations is selected.

Recovery Version (RV)

When the Recovery Version is selected, a live request is made to api.lsm.org, operated by Living Stream Ministry, to fetch the current verse. Only the single most recently fetched verse is cached, locally on your device — nothing is sent to any server operated by this app.

Local Storage

Your settings, favorites, memorization progress, and the most recently loaded verse(s) are stored locally in an App Group container (group.com.rippre.wordunlocked), shared only between Word Unlocked and its Lock Screen widget on the same device. This data is never uploaded or shared externally.

Third-Party SDKs

This app contains no third-party SDKs of any kind — no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no social login.

Children's Privacy

Word Unlocked does not collect personal data from any user, including children. Because no data collection occurs anywhere in the app, none occurs for users of any age.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date above.
