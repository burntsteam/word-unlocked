Support

Word Unlocked puts a Bible verse on your iPhone Lock Screen. Pick a mode — Daily Verse, Weekly Plan, Topic, Chapter, Memorization, or Favorites — and a translation, and the widget keeps your Lock Screen updated with scripture. Five translations (KJV, WEB, BSB, ASV, LSV) work fully offline; the English Standard Version downloads the verses you’ll see next, and the Recovery Version loads while you read.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add the Lock Screen widget?

Lock your iPhone, then touch and hold the Lock Screen until "Customize" appears. Tap Customize, tap the Lock Screen, then tap the widget area below the clock. Find "Word Unlocked" in the widget list and tap it to add it, then tap Done.

Why isn't my verse updating?

iOS manages exactly when widgets refresh to save battery, so updates aren't always instant. First check Settings in the app to confirm you're in the mode and translation you expect — modes like Daily Verse, Topic, and Chapter each have their own update or rotation interval. If the English Standard Version is selected, new verses download at most once every 48 hours, and a verse that hasn’t downloaded yet shows in the King James Version. The Recovery Version can’t be stored on your device, so while it’s selected the widget shows the King James Version.

How do I switch translations?

Open the app and go to Settings, then use the Default Translation picker (or the Translations tab to browse all available translations). KJV, WEB, BSB, ASV, and LSV are available immediately since they're bundled offline. Selecting the English Standard Version downloads the verses your mode shows next; the Recovery Version loads each verse while you read.

How does offline mode work?

King James Version, World English Bible, Berean Standard Bible, American Standard Version, and Literal Standard Version are bundled inside the app itself, so no network request is ever made when one of them is selected. The English Standard Version keeps up to 500 downloaded verses on your device and needs a connection only to download more, at most once every 48 hours. The Recovery Version needs a connection whenever you read it, because its text is never stored on your device.
